Retention does not start on the day someone asks to cancel. Sometimes it starts weeks earlier, when a member stops coming in, delays a renewal or loses touch with the team.

A gym does not need to message everyone all the time. It needs to notice changes in routine and organize follow-up that feels personal.

## 1. Look at frequency, not only the last visit

A member who used to visit three times a week and has not shown up for ten days has a different change in routine from someone who trains once every two weeks. Choose a reasonable period and compare recent activity with their usual frequency.

Attendance changes for holidays, illness and work too. Treat the signal as a reason to ask, not a conclusion about why someone stopped coming.

## 2. Add membership context

The last visit is more useful when reviewed alongside membership status: due date, plan, payments and bookings. This helps the team distinguish a short break from a renewal that needs attention.

Keeping those details in one profile helps reception and administration work from the same context, even across different shifts.

## 3. Reach out simply and at the right time

A short message can ask how training is going or whether the member needs help getting back into a routine. Avoid assuming why they have been away or pressuring them into an automatic renewal.

If the member replies, record the follow-up and the next step. If they ask not to be contacted, respect that preference.

## 4. Review what works

Track which group received follow-up, through which channel and what happened next. Over time, you can learn which actions help members return to a routine and which ones create noise.

Use the information to improve the gym experience and keep it protected. Limit access to contact details and store only what you need to operate.

## Build a process the team can maintain

Choose a review cadence, decide who handles follow-up and avoid creating lists nobody updates. A small, consistent process is usually more useful than a large campaign that runs once.
